Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Transparent answers about our motorcycle services, workshop timings, pricing policy, and location in Coimbatore.

Where is Boss Moto Expertz located in Coimbatore?
Boss Moto Expertz is conveniently located at 42, Amarar Jeeva Main Road, Nalvar Layout, Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu. We are centrally positioned with quick connectivity to Avinashi Road, Peelamedu, and Gandhipuram.
What motorcycle brands do you service at your workshop?
We service all major Indian and international motorcycle manufacturers, including Royal Enfield, KTM, Yamaha, Honda, TVS, Bajaj, Kawasaki, Suzuki, BMW Motorrad, Triumph, and Jawa. We also specialize in vintage 2-stroke restorations (Yamaha RX100, RD350).
Do you offer superbike ECU tuning and diagnostics in Coimbatore?
Yes. Our certified electrical technicians utilize advanced OBD and ECU diagnostic scanners for sensor error clearing, fuel-map calibration, and throttle response enhancement across twin, triple, and four-cylinder performance motorcycles.
Are you open on Sundays?
Our regular workshop operating hours are Monday through Saturday: 9:00 AM – 7:00 PM. On Sundays, service is strictly based on prior appointment to support weekend emergency maintenance, long-distance ride preparations, and custom project deliveries.
How are service estimates calculated?
Unlike workshops with rigid packages, our estimates are based on vehicle condition, genuine OEM parts needed, and actual mechanical labour required. We complete a thorough physical inspection and explain all recommended jobs with transparent estimates before beginning any work.
How do I book a service appointment?
You can book directly online via our Book Service page, contact us via WhatsApp at +91 63 799 299 63, or call our Coimbatore workshop team directly.
