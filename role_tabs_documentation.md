# EduTech Platform: Role & Tab Documentation

This document provides a detailed breakdown of the navigation tabs and features available to each user role within the EduTech application. The platform is designed with distinct interfaces tailored to the specific needs of Students, Teachers, Parents, and Administrators.

---

## 🎓 Student

The Student workspace is focused on learning, engagement, and AI-assisted support.

*   **Dashboard** (`/student/dashboard`)
    *   **Use:** The central hub providing a quick overview of upcoming live classes, pending assignments, recent grades, and daily study goals.
*   **Live Class** (`/student/smart-live-classroom`)
    *   **Use:** Allows students to join scheduled, interactive live video sessions with their teachers. Includes features for raising hands and participating in polls.
*   **Classwork** (`/student/classwork`)
    *   **Use:** The primary area for viewing assigned homework, submitting completed work, and accessing day-to-day study materials provided by the teacher.
*   **Assessments** (`/student/assessments`)
    *   **Use:** Dedicated space for taking formal quizzes, tests, and tracking performance scores over time.
*   **Socratic AI Tutor** (`/student/ai-tutor`)
    *   **Use:** An interactive AI assistant that helps students learn by asking guiding questions rather than just giving the answers, fostering critical thinking.
*   **AI Psychologist** (`/student/ai-psychologist`)
    *   **Use:** A private, AI-driven support tool designed to help students manage stress, test anxiety, and support their overall mental well-being.
*   **Courses** (`/student/courses`)
    *   **Use:** A library where students can browse all their enrolled subjects, syllabi, and structured course content.
*   **Study Planner** (`/student/study-planner`)
    *   **Use:** A calendar and scheduling tool helping students manage their time, plan study sessions, and set reminders for upcoming deadlines.
*   **Mock Exam** (`/student/mock-exam`)
    *   **Use:** A practice testing environment where students can take simulated exams to prepare for real assessments.
*   **Gamification** (`/student/gamification`)
    *   **Use:** Displays the student's earned badges, achievements, points, and leaderboards to keep them motivated and engaged.
*   **Recorded Classes** (`/student/recorded-classes`)
    *   **Use:** An archive of past live sessions, allowing students to re-watch lessons for review or catch up on missed classes.

## 👩‍🏫 Teacher

The Teacher workspace provides tools for class management, content delivery, and student monitoring.

*   **Dashboard** (`/teacher/dashboard`)
    *   **Use:** Overview of the day's schedule, pending grading tasks, quick alerts, and high-level class performance metrics.
*   **Live Class** (`/teacher/live-class`)
    *   **Use:** The control center for starting and managing live interactive sessions, sharing screens, and monitoring student participation.
*   **Classwork** (`/teacher/classwork`)
    *   **Use:** Allows teachers to create assignments, distribute materials, grade student submissions, and provide feedback.
*   **Course Notes** (`/teacher/course-notes`)
    *   **Use:** A dedicated tool for creating, organizing, and distributing study notes and lecture summaries to the class.
*   **Assessments** (`/teacher/content`)
    *   **Use:** The creation suite for building quizzes, tests, and managing the overall learning content repository.
*   **Analytics** (`/teacher/analytics`)
    *   **Use:** In-depth data visualization showing class-wide performance trends, attendance rates, and areas where students are struggling.
*   **Center Alerts** (`/teacher/alerts`)
    *   **Use:** A communication channel for receiving important administrative alerts, compliance warnings, or notices from center management.
*   **Students** (`/teacher/students`)
    *   **Use:** The class roster. Allows teachers to view individual student profiles, track specific progress, and communicate directly.
*   **Recorded Lessons** (`/teacher/recorded-lessons`)
    *   **Use:** Management interface for reviewing, editing, and publishing recordings of past live classes for student access.

## 👨‍👩‍👧 Parent

The Parent workspace focuses on transparency, progress tracking, and communication.

*   **Dashboard** (`/parent/dashboard`)
    *   **Use:** A unified view summarizing the activities, attendance, and recent grades of all their children enrolled in the platform.
*   **Child Progress** (`/parent/child-detail`)
    *   **Use:** Detailed drill-down into a specific child's academic performance, AI tutor usage, behavior, and strengths/weaknesses.
*   **Center Alerts** (`/parent/alerts`)
    *   **Use:** Critical notifications from the school or learning center regarding closures, events, or urgent policy updates.
*   **Messages** (`/parent/messages`)
    *   **Use:** A direct messaging system to communicate securely with teachers or center administrators regarding the child.
*   **Notifications** (`/parent/notifications`)
    *   **Use:** A feed of day-to-day updates, such as when a child completes an assignment, earns a badge, or misses a class.

## 🏢 Center Mgr (Center Manager)

The Center Manager workspace is designed for operational oversight at the individual learning center level.

*   **Dashboard** (`/admin/dashboard`)
    *   **Use:** High-level summary of center operations, active user counts, revenue (if applicable), and critical daily metrics.
*   **Users** (`/admin/users`)
    *   **Use:** Comprehensive directory to manage all accounts (Students, Teachers, Parents) within their specific center. Includes password resets and role assignments.
*   **Monitoring** (`/admin/monitoring`)
    *   **Use:** Real-time oversight tool allowing managers to check active live classes, monitor system health, and observe center activities.
*   **Analytics** (`/admin/analytics`)
    *   **Use:** Center-wide data reporting on student outcomes, teacher performance, attendance, and overall engagement metrics.
*   **Trust & Safety** (`/admin/compliance`)
    *   **Use:** The compliance hub for managing incident reports, reviewing automated policy violations (e.g., inappropriate chat behavior), and enforcing safety rules.
*   **Feedback Corner** (`/admin/feedback`)
    *   **Use:** A review system for reading and responding to feedback, complaints, or suggestions submitted by students, parents, and teachers.
*   **Approvals** (`/admin/approvals`)
    *   **Use:** A queue for reviewing and approving pending actions, such as new account registrations, profile change requests, or content publishing.

---

## 🌍 Dist Admin & State Admin (District / State Administrators)

The District and State Admin workspaces provide macro-level oversight across multiple centers or entire regions.

*   **Dashboard** (`/admin/dashboard`)
    *   **Use:** A macro-level command center showing aggregated metrics across all districts, schools, or centers under their jurisdiction.
*   **District Admins / Center Managers (Users)** (`/admin/users`)
    *   **Use:** User management focused on administrative staff. Used to onboard, manage, and oversee the Center Managers or District Admins reporting to them.
*   **Analytics** (`/admin/analytics`)
    *   **Use:** Large-scale data aggregation tools to compare performance between different centers, track regional trends, and generate official reports.
*   **Trust & Safety** (`/admin/compliance`)
    *   **Use:** High-level monitoring of severe compliance issues, systemic safety concerns, and regional policy enforcement.
*   **Approvals** (`/admin/approvals`)
    *   **Use:** Administrative queue for high-level approvals, such as authorizing new centers, approving major curriculum changes, or resolving escalated disputes.

### ⚙️ Global / Utility Features (Available to All Roles)
*   **Global Search (Command Palette - `Ctrl+K`):** Instantly search for users, courses, settings, or jump to specific pages across the app.
*   **Theme Toggle:** Switch between Light and Dark mode for visual comfort.
*   **Language Toggle:** Switch the platform interface language (e.g., English / Tamil).
*   **Notification Bell:** Phone-style popup banner alerts and a panel for unread messages, compliance warnings, or updates.
*   **Profile Menu:** Accessible via the avatar in the top right. Allows users to view/edit their personal details and manage account settings.
